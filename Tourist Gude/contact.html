FAQ

Common Questions

🛂 Do I need a visa to visit Kenya?

Kenya offers an eVisa for most nationalities, applied for online before travel. East African Community citizens (Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, Burundi, South Sudan) enjoy visa-free entry. Check the eCitizen portal for current requirements.

🌦️ What is the best time to visit Kenya?

Kenya is a year-round destination. The dry seasons (Jan–Feb and Jun–Oct) are best for wildlife viewing and the Great Migration. The coastal beaches are beautiful all year, with the coolest and driest conditions from Jun–Aug.

🚌 How do I get around Kenya?

Domestic flights connect Nairobi to the Mara, Mombasa, Malindi, and other destinations quickly. Matatus (minibuses) and buses serve intercity routes affordably. For safaris, most lodges organise transfers from Nairobi or the nearest airstrip.

💳 What currency does Kenya use?

Kenya uses the Kenyan Shilling (KSh). M-Pesa mobile money is widely accepted. Major hotels and tourist facilities accept credit cards. It's worth carrying some cash for local markets, matatus, and smaller establishments.
