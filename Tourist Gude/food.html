Taste Kenya

Food & Dining

From nyama choma roadside grills to Swahili seafood feasts — Kenya's food culture is bold, diverse, and delicious.

📷 Add Image Here Carnivore Restaurant, Nairobi src="images/carnivore-nairobi.jpg"
Iconic Restaurant

Carnivore Restaurant, Nairobi

One of Africa's most famous dining experiences, Carnivore has been serving legendary nyama choma (roasted meats) in Nairobi since 1980. Meats are roasted on Maasai swords over a giant charcoal pit and carved at your table in an endless feast.

Located in Langata near Nairobi National Park, this is a bucket-list dining experience for any visitor to Kenya. The all-you-can-eat format includes game meats alongside the classics.

  • 📍 Langata Road, Nairobi
  • 🕘 Daily: Lunch 12PM–3PM  |  Dinner 6PM–10:30PM
  • 💰 From KSh 4,500 per person (all-you-can-eat)
  • ⭐ Must-try: Crocodile and ostrich swords

Coastal Cuisine

Swahili Cuisine, Mombasa Old Town

Mombasa's Old Town is a treasure trove of authentic Swahili cooking influenced by Arab, Indian, and Portuguese flavours. Dishes like biryani, pilau, coconut fish curry, and maharagwe (coconut bean stew) are staples at family-run restaurants lining the narrow streets.

Don't miss a breakfast of maandazi (fried doughnuts) and chai at one of the open-air tea houses near the Old Port. The evening street food scene around Mtwapa is also world-class.

  • 📍 Old Town & Mtwapa, Mombasa
  • 🕘 Most open 7:00 AM – 10:00 PM daily
  • 💰 Mains from KSh 400 — great value
  • ⭐ Must-try: Coconut fish curry with rice

Local Favourite

Mama Oliech Restaurant, Nairobi

A legendary Nairobi institution loved by presidents, politicians, and everyday Kenyans alike. Mama Oliech specialises in deep-fried tilapia from Lake Victoria served with ugali (maize meal), sukuma wiki (collard greens), and kachumbari (tomato salsa).

The no-frills atmosphere and generous portions make it one of the most authentic local dining experiences in the city. Expect queues at lunchtime — they are very much worth the wait.

  • 📍 Hurlingham, Nairobi
  • 🕘 Daily: 11:00 AM – 9:00 PM
  • 💰 Full meal from KSh 600 — excellent value
  • ⭐ Must-try: Fried tilapia with ugali

Fine Dining

Ali Barbour's Cave Restaurant, Diani

One of Kenya's most unique dining venues — a 180,000-year-old coral cave on the Diani Beach strip that serves outstanding seafood under a natural open-air roof revealing the stars above. Lobster, prawns, and catch-of-the-day are prepared with French and Swahili influences.

Reservations are essential. The cave fits only a small number of tables, making this an intimate and unforgettable experience, especially on a full moon night.

  • 📍 Diani Beach Road, Kwale County
  • 🕘 Dinner only: Tue–Sun 7:00 PM – 11:00 PM
  • 💰 From KSh 5,000 per person
  • 📞 Reservations essential: +254 040 320 2033
