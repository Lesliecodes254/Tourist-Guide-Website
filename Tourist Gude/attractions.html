Attractions

From world-renowned wildlife reserves to ancient coastal cities — Kenya's attractions are truly extraordinary.

Wildlife

Maasai Mara National Reserve

Arguably Africa's greatest wildlife sanctuary, the Maasai Mara covers 1,510 km² of rolling savannah in southwest Kenya. It is most famous for the annual Great Wildebeest Migration (July–October), when over 1.5 million wildebeest, zebras, and gazelles cross from Tanzania's Serengeti.

Year-round you can spot lions, elephants, cheetahs, leopards, and buffalo. Hot air balloon safaris at dawn offer a magical bird's-eye view of the plains.

  • 📍 Narok County, Southwest Kenya
  • 🕘 Open year-round; best Jul–Oct for migration
  • 🎟️ Non-residents: $200/day  |  Residents: KSh 1,200/day
  • ⏱ Recommended stay: 2–5 days

Wildlife

Amboseli National Park

Amboseli is one of Kenya's most visited parks, offering iconic views of Africa's highest peak, Mount Kilimanjaro, as a backdrop to vast elephant herds. The park's open marshlands and swamps attract large concentrations of wildlife year-round.

Amboseli is also home to Maasai communities who offer cultural village visits and guided walking safaris sharing their deep knowledge of the land.

  • 📍 Kajiado County, 240 km from Nairobi
  • 🕘 Open daily: 6:00 AM – 7:00 PM
  • 🎟️ Non-residents: $60/day  |  Residents: KSh 400/day
  • ⏱ Recommended stay: 1–3 days

History

Fort Jesus, Mombasa

A UNESCO World Heritage Site, Fort Jesus was built by the Portuguese in 1593 on Mombasa Island. This massive coral stone fortress stands as one of the finest examples of 16th-century Portuguese military architecture in the world.

Today it houses the Mombasa Museum with fascinating exhibits on Swahili coastal history, Arab traders, Portuguese colonialism, and the East African slave trade.

  • 📍 Old Town, Mombasa
  • 🕘 Daily: 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM
  • 🎟️ Adults: KSh 1,200  |  Children: KSh 200
  • ⏱ Recommended visit: 1–2 hours

Adventure

Hell's Gate National Park

One of Kenya's most unique parks, Hell's Gate allows visitors to walk, cycle, and rock-climb among wildlife without a vehicle. Dramatic gorges, geothermal steam vents, towering cliffs, and resident wildlife including zebras, giraffes, and baboons make it unlike any other park.

The gorge walk through Fischer's Tower and Central Tower is a must-do. The park also inspired the scenery in the animated film The Lion King.

  • 📍 Naivasha, Nakuru County
  • 🕘 Daily: 6:00 AM – 6:00 PM
  • 🎟️ Non-residents: $26/day  |  Residents: KSh 215/day
  • ⏱ Recommended visit: Full day
