Iconic destinations that make Kenya one of Africa's most remarkable countries to visit.

Wildlife

Maasai Mara National Reserve

Kenya's most famous wildlife reserve. Witness the Great Migration and encounter the Big Five on unforgettable game drives across the golden plains.

📍 Narok County  ·  ⏱ 2–5 Days

Wildlife

Amboseli National Park

Famous for its large elephant herds and stunning views of Mount Kilimanjaro rising beyond the savannah. One of Africa's most scenic parks.

📍 Kajiado County  ·  ⏱ 1–3 Days
📷 Add Image Here Diani Beach src="images/diani-beach.jpg"
Beach

Diani Beach

Voted Africa's leading beach destination multiple times. White coral sand, warm turquoise waters, water sports, and swaying coconut palms.

📍 Kwale County  ·  ⏱ 2–7 Days
📷 Add Image Here Mount Kenya src="images/mount-kenya.jpg"
Adventure

Mount Kenya National Park

Africa's second highest peak and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Trekking routes for all levels wind through moorlands and bamboo forests.

📍 Central Kenya  ·  ⏱ 3–6 Days
📷 Add Image Here Nairobi National Museum src="images/nairobi-museum.jpg"
Culture

Nairobi National Museum

Kenya's leading cultural and heritage museum. Housing prehistoric fossils, natural history, Kenyan art, and cultural exhibits across its galleries.

📍 Museum Hill, Nairobi  ·  ⏱ 2–3 hrs
📷 Add Image Here Lake Nakuru src="images/lake-nakuru.jpg"
Nature

Lake Nakuru National Park

A flamingo-fringed soda lake set in a spectacular national park. Home to rhinos, lions, leopards, and over 450 bird species.

📍 Nakuru County  ·  ⏱ 1–2 Days
