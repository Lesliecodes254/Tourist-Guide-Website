Where to Stay

From luxury tented camps on the Mara plains to boutique coastal retreats — Kenya offers stays as spectacular as its scenery.

📷 Add Image Here Angama Mara, Maasai Mara src="images/angama-mara.jpg"
Luxury Safari Lodge

Angama Mara, Maasai Mara

Perched on the edge of the Great Rift Valley escarpment 500 metres above the Maasai Mara, Angama Mara is consistently ranked among Africa's finest safari lodges. Its 30 tented suites offer sweeping 180° views across the plains below.

Inspired by the film Out of Africa, the lodge features a full spa, photography studio, Maasai cultural experiences, and twice-daily game drives into the reserve. All meals and activities are included.

  • 📍 Oloololo Escarpment, Maasai Mara
  • 💰 From $1,200 per person per night (full board)
  • ⭐ Rating: 5-star  ·  Condé Nast Top 10 Africa
  • 📞 reservations@angama.com
📷 Add Image Here Sarova Whitesands, Mombasa src="images/sarova-whitesands.jpg"
Beach Resort

Sarova Whitesands Beach Resort, Mombasa

Set on the pristine white sands of Bamburi Beach north of Mombasa, Sarova Whitesands is one of Kenya's most beloved coastal resorts. The expansive property features five swimming pools, multiple restaurants, a full water sports centre, and lush tropical gardens.

The resort is ideal for families, with a dedicated kids' club, shallow pools, and a wide beach perfect for water activities. Dhow sunset cruises depart from the resort's beach every evening.

  • 📍 Bamburi Beach, Mombasa North Coast
  • 💰 From KSh 18,000 per room per night
  • ⭐ Rating: 4-star  ·  TripAdvisor: 4.5/5
  • 📞 +254 041 548 5000
📷 Add Image Here Tribe Hotel, Nairobi src="images/tribe-hotel.jpg"
Boutique City Hotel

Tribe Hotel, Nairobi

A design-led boutique hotel in the upscale Village Market precinct in Gigiri, Tribe Hotel blends African art and craftsmanship with contemporary luxury. Each of the 137 rooms is uniquely decorated with African artwork and handcrafted furniture.

The award-winning The Market restaurant serves innovative Kenyan-influenced cuisine, while the rooftop pool and spa offer a serene escape from the city. Close to the UN complex and Safari Park Hotel.

  • 📍 Village Market, Gigiri, Nairobi
  • 💰 From KSh 22,000 per room per night
  • ⭐ Rating: 5-star boutique  ·  Loved on TripAdvisor
  • 📞 +254 020 720 0000
📷 Add Image Here Wildebeest Eco Camp, Nairobi src="images/wildebeest-camp.jpg"
Budget-Friendly

Wildebeest Eco Camp, Nairobi

A firm favourite among backpackers and budget travellers, Wildebeest Eco Camp offers a beautiful, eco-conscious stay in a lush garden setting in Kibera, Nairobi. Options range from dormitory bunks to private bandas (cottages) and camping pitches.

The communal fire pit, on-site bar, solar power, and rainwater harvesting give it a genuine eco feel. Staff are incredibly helpful with organising onward safari bookings to all major parks.

  • 📍 Kibera Drive, Nairobi
  • 💰 Dorms from KSh 1,500  |  Bandas from KSh 5,000
  • ⭐ Hostelworld: 9.1/10 — Exceptional
  • 📞 +254 020 261 8051
